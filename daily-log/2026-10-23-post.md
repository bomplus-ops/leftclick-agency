## Post Text

We screened 47 applicants in 4 hours.

No one on our team read a single resume.

Here's the exact pipeline we built for a 12-person agency hiring a senior ops manager:

**Before:**
- 8–10 hours per hire cycle reading resumes
- Inconsistent scoring (whoever reviewed last set the bar)
- Best candidates ghosted during 3-day manual review lag
- 2 bad hires in the previous 12 months at ~$22k each in wasted ramp costs

**After:**
- Typeform application with role-specific screening questions
- Make parses every submission and sends to GPT-4o
- AI scores each applicant on 6 criteria (experience, culture fit, red flags, communication quality, role alignment, portfolio evidence)
- Shortlist of top 8 with scores + reasoning delivered to Slack in 40 minutes
- Team reviews 8 summaries (not 47 resumes) — 45 minutes total

**The stack:**
- Typeform for application intake
- Make for orchestration
- GPT-4o for scoring and reasoning
- Slack for shortlist delivery
- Notion for candidate tracking

Total build time: 6 hours.
Hours saved per hire: ~9.
Ramp cost avoided (fewer bad hires): estimated $22k+.
