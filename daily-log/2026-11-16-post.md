**Body:**

Here's what it looks like inside:

1. Sales call ends → Typeform captures the brief automatically
2. Make pulls the data and sends it to GPT-4o with a custom prompt trained on our best proposals
3. GPT-4o generates the full proposal draft: scope, pricing tier, timeline, deliverables
4. Docupilot formats it into a branded PDF — logo, colors, fonts, everything
5. Slack pings the account manager: "Your proposal is ready in Drive."

Total time from brief to polished PDF: **12 minutes.**
Human touch: **review + send.**

The result for one of our agency clients:
→ 10 proposals/month → 10 proposals/week
→ Proposal turnaround: 4h → 12 min
→ Close rate: up 18% (more time to refine positioning, less time formatting)
