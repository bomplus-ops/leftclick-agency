## Post Text

We built a cold email system for a B2B SaaS client.

Before: 1.2% reply rate. 87 emails sent per week, by hand. 1 meeting per month if they were lucky.

After: 4.7% reply rate. 340 emails per week, automated. 4× the meetings — without adding headcount.

Here's what actually changed:

**It wasn't the volume.**

Most people think cold outreach fails because of volume. Send more, get more replies. So they buy another list, blast another sequence, and wonder why reply rates drop further.

Volume is not the problem. Generic is the problem.

A prospect can smell a templated email in 0.3 seconds. The moment they see "Hi {{FirstName}}, I noticed you work at {{Company}}" they're already hitting delete.

**What we changed:**

1. **Personalization at the line level, not the template level.**
   We built a Make pipeline that pulls each prospect's recent LinkedIn activity, company news, and role-specific pain points. GPT-4o writes a unique first line for every single email — not a variable, an actual observation. "Saw your team just launched a new onboarding flow — guessing manual follow-up is eating your AEs' time."

2. **Offer precision.**
   Generic offer = generic response. We rewrote the CTA to target the exact bottleneck for each ICP segment. SDRs got one offer. Founders got another. Each felt like it was written for them — because it was.

3. **Deliverability infrastructure first.**
   Warmup sequences. Domain rotation. Sending windows matched to each prospect's timezone. The best email in the world doesn't matter if it lands in spam.

The result: 340 outreach touchpoints per week, fully automated, each one personalized. The sales team's only job is to reply when a prospect says yes.

**Cold outreach isn't dead. Lazy cold outreach is dead.**

If your team is still manually writing emails one at a time, or using the same template for every prospect in your list — you're competing with AI-personalized sequences and losing on attention before the reply button is even visible.

The gap between teams using AI-personalized outreach and teams not using it is widening every quarter.

Happy to show you what this looks like for your specific ICP. Link in bio.
