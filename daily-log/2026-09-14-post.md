## Body

Here's exactly how we built it:

**The old workflow:**
- Sales call ends
- Account manager opens Google Docs
- Spends 2–4 hours writing a custom proposal
- Pastes in screenshots, reformats pricing, edits tone
- Sends. Waits. Follows up manually.

**The new workflow:**
- Sales call ends
- AE fills in a 5-field Typeform (client name, industry, pain point, budget range, timeline)
- Make pulls their CRM data + call notes
- GPT-4o generates a branded, personalized proposal in their exact voice
- PDF is auto-generated, dropped in Drive, and emailed to the prospect in 3 minutes

**Results after 60 days:**
- Proposal turnaround: 4 hours → 3 minutes
- Proposals sent per week: 3 → 11 (same team, no extra hires)
- Close rate: flat — quality held
- AE time reclaimed: 12h/week redirected to calls

The bottleneck wasn't quality. It was friction.

Tools used: Make, GPT-4o, Typeform, Google Drive, Docupilot, Gmail
