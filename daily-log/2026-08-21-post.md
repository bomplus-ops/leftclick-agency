Most agencies send cold emails.

90% of them get ignored.

Here's what separates the 10% that book meetings:

---

We built an AI cold outreach system for a B2B SaaS client earlier this year.

Before: 200 manual emails/week. 1.2% reply rate. 2–3 meetings/month.

After (6 weeks in):
↳ 800 personalized emails/week (AI-written, human-reviewed)
↳ 4.7% reply rate
↳ 12–14 meetings/month

Same offer. Same list. 4× the output. 4× the meetings.

---

The difference wasn't the volume.

It was personalization at scale.

Each email referenced:
• The prospect's recent LinkedIn post or company news
• A specific pain point mapped to their industry and role
• A one-line connection to a relevant client result

An AI agent researched each prospect in ~40 seconds. Then wrote a first draft. Then a human spent 5 seconds spot-checking.

Result: emails that felt hand-written. Speed that felt impossible.

---

The hidden win nobody talks about:

When your reply rate goes from 1.2% → 4.7%, your cost per booked meeting drops 75%.

That means you can test more offers, more segments, more angles — without burning through your list.

Volume buys you data. Personalization turns data into deals.
