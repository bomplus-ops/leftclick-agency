We ran a 3-day ops audit for a 12-person agency.

Found 14 hours/week in recoverable time.

Here are the 4 automations we built:

**1. Client onboarding → automated**
New contract signed → Zapier fires → project created in ClickUp, client added to Slack, onboarding doc sent, invoice scheduled. Zero manual steps. 3h/week recovered.

**2. Reporting → automated**
Every Monday at 8am: n8n pulls data from 6 sources, builds a PDF, emails it to each client with their name and logo. The founder stopped spending Sunday nights on this. 4h/week recovered.

**3. Lead follow-up → automated**
New inbound lead → AI drafts a personalized reply using their website + LinkedIn → queued in Instantly for human review → sent in 8 minutes. Response rate went up. 3h/week recovered.

**4. Proposal generation → automated**
Sales call notes → Claude summarizes pain points → fills a proposal template → sends to Notion for review. First draft done before the call ends. 4h/week recovered.

Total: 14 hours. Every week. Forever.

That's 56 hours/month the team now spends on client work instead of operations.
