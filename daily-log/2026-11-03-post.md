A client's onboarding process looked like this:

New client signs contract.
Account manager gets a Slack ping.
Account manager opens a Google Doc template, copies it, renames it.
Creates a ClickUp project from a template.
Sends a 12-field intake form manually by email.
Waits for responses.
Copies responses into the project.
Sends the welcome email.
Schedules the kickoff call.

3 hours. Every. Single. Time.

They had 4 new clients per month. That's 12 hours of senior-team time on copy-paste, not on actually serving the clients.

We wired up Typeform → Make → ClickUp → Gmail.

Now it's:
→ Client signs contract → Typeform link fires automatically
→ Client fills intake form at their convenience
→ Make parses responses, creates the ClickUp project, populates all fields
→ Branded welcome email goes out with kickoff link
→ Account manager gets a Slack summary with everything pre-filled

0 manual steps. 0 minutes. Client experience is better because nothing gets dropped.

4 clients/month: 12 hours recovered. The team runs 4× the capacity now.

The best onboarding process is the one that runs without you.
