## Draft

We screened 200 applicants in 40 minutes.

Not with a recruiter.
Not with a VA.
With a Make scenario, a Google Form, and a GPT-4o scoring rubric.

Here's the exact 5-step pipeline we built for a 12-person agency drowning in resumes:

**Step 1 — Intake form**
Google Form captures: role applied for, years of experience, a 200-word "why us" response, and a portfolio link. Nothing fancy.

**Step 2 — Make triggers on submission**
Every new form response fires a Make webhook. The scenario extracts the structured fields automatically.

**Step 3 — GPT-4o scores against a rubric**
We pass the response data to GPT-4o with a role-specific rubric: 10 criteria, weighted by importance. Culture fit, skill match, communication quality, red flags. GPT-4o returns a score (0–100) and 3-sentence summary for each candidate.

**Step 4 — Ranked shortlist auto-populated**
Scores and summaries write directly to a Google Sheet, sorted highest to lowest. Top 15% get flagged automatically. No manual sorting, no missed applicants.

**Step 5 — Human makes the final call**
The hiring manager opens one spreadsheet, reads 15 summaries, and books interviews with the top 5. Total human time: ~20 minutes.

Full process: 40 minutes from close-of-applications to interview calendar invites sent.

Before: 2-week hiring process, 3 people involved, inevitable bias toward familiar names at the top of the inbox.

After: 40 minutes. One person. Evidence-based ranking.

We built this for $0 in added tooling cost (they already had Make and a GPT-4o API key).
