We helped a 20-person agency screen 200 applicants for an ops role.

Their old process: 3 days, 3 people, still missed the best candidates.

With AI: 40 minutes. One person. Better shortlist.

---

Here's what the system actually does:

Each application goes through an AI scoring layer that evaluates:
• Specific experience match (not just job titles — actual task overlap)
• Writing clarity and communication quality
• Red flag detection (job hopping patterns, vague claims, mismatched timelines)
• Culture fit signals based on how they answered the "why this role" question

Output: a ranked shortlist with a 1-page summary per candidate.

The hiring manager reviews the top 12 in 20 minutes instead of reading 200 applications.

---

What surprised the client most:

The AI flagged two candidates who looked great on paper but gave near-identical answers to the open-ended questions — a clear sign of ChatGPT-assisted applications.

Both were removed from the shortlist. The final hire was someone who ranked #4 on the AI list but wrote a genuinely specific, detailed answer about their ops experience.

The AI found the signal. The human made the call.

---

Bad hires cost 3–5× the role's annual salary when you factor in ramp time, lost productivity, and the rehire cycle.

Most agencies still screen manually because "it's always been that way."

The ones building AI-assisted hiring systems are shortlisting faster, hiring better, and spending the time they save on actual growth work.
