**Body:**

The audit took 90 minutes. We asked one question for every task:

*"Does a human need to make a decision here, or just move information from one place to another?"*

The 4 things that failed the test:

**1. Project status updates**
Every Friday, an account manager spent 90 min pulling updates from ClickUp, writing a summary, and emailing 6 clients.
→ Now: Make pulls task completion data at 4:55pm Friday → GPT-4o writes the client summary → Gmail sends it at 5pm.
Time saved: **6h/week**

**2. New client onboarding**
Sales closed a deal → ops had to manually create the ClickUp project, send the welcome email, schedule the kickoff, and add the client to Slack.
→ Now: HubSpot deal marked "Closed Won" → Zapier triggers project creation + email + Calendly link in one sequence.
Time saved: **3h/week**

**3. Invoice follow-up**
One person's entire Tuesday was chasing unpaid invoices.
→ Now: Stripe webhook → overdue flag → Make sends a polite follow-up email at day 3, day 7, day 14. Human only steps in at day 21.
Time saved: **3h/week**

**4. Social proof collection**
Asking clients for testimonials was awkward and always got deprioritized.
→ Now: Typeform sends automatically at project close → responses feed a Notion "Testimonials" database → flagged in Slack for use.
Time saved: **2h/week**

Total: **14h/week recovered.** That's 56h/month. For a 7-person team, that's nearly one full person's time.

The work isn't complex. The systems are $80/month in tools.

The bottleneck isn't cost. It's knowing which 4 things to target.
