**Body:**

Here's how it works:

→ **Typeform** collects structured intake: role fit questions, compensation expectations, work sample prompts
→ **Make** passes every submission to **GPT-4o** with a custom scoring rubric (we build it with the client — usually 6–8 criteria weighted by role priority)
→ GPT-4o returns a score, a 3-line rationale, and a flag: Interview / Hold / Pass
→ **Notion** gets a ranked shortlist. **Slack** pings the hiring manager with the top 5.

The human spends 15 minutes reviewing 5 pre-screened candidates instead of 4 hours wading through 200 raw applications.

For one client: 3 open roles filled in 11 days. Down from 6 weeks average.
