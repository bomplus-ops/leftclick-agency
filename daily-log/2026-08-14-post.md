## Hook

Your team is rewriting the same proposal from scratch every time. Here's how we stopped that.

---

## Beat 1 — BEFORE: the proposal tax

Every new client pitch at most agencies starts with someone opening a Google Doc and recreating a proposal structure they've built 40 times before. The case study section pulls from memory or an old deck. The pricing section is copy-pasted from the last client who was "sort of similar." The deliverables list gets rewritten from scratch.

A sales or account manager at a mid-size agency might spend 4–6 hours on a single proposal. Multiply by the number of active deals in the pipeline, and you have a senior person spending the equivalent of 1–2 full days per week producing documents that are 80% identical to each other.

We saw this with an agency client closing 8–12 deals per month. Their proposal process: manually assemble scope, write a custom case study section, format pricing, get it reviewed, send. Average time: 5 hours per proposal. 8 proposals/month = 40 hours of senior account manager time every month on document production.

---

## Beat 2 — WHAT WE BUILT

An AI asset generator that turns a 10-minute intake form into a complete, client-ready proposal:

1. **Intake form**: account manager fills in client industry, pain points, desired outcomes, deal size, and selects 2–3 relevant case studies from a dropdown
2. **AI draft generation**: a Claude prompt compiles the intake data, pulls from a curated case study library, and generates a full proposal — scope, deliverables, timeline, pricing table, and a custom "why us" section referencing the selected case studies
3. **Review layer**: the draft lands in a shared Google Doc formatted and ready — account manager does a 20-minute review pass, adjusts tone, adds any deal-specific notes
4. **Output**: proposal ready to send in under 30 minutes total, consistently structured, client-specific content in every section

The case study library is maintained once — new results get added, and every future proposal can reference them automatically.

---

## Beat 3 — RESULT

Proposal production time: 5 hours → 30 minutes. Account manager reviews the output instead of building from scratch. In the first month, the agency sent 14 proposals instead of 8 — not because they hired someone, but because the bottleneck was removed.

The proposals also got better. Consistent structure, relevant case studies pulled every time, no sections left at a previous client's pricing because "copy-paste got away from me."

---

## Close

The limitation isn't your team's writing ability. It's a system that requires them to write from zero every single time.

An AI asset generator doesn't replace judgment — it removes the blank page. Your team makes the calls; the system does the assembly.
