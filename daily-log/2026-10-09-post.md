We went from 1.2% to 4.7% reply rate on cold outreach.
Same list. Same offer. Different system.

Before: manually personalized 30–40 emails/day. Burned out after 2 weeks. Results inconsistent.

After: built an AI personalization pipeline in Make:
→ Lead pulled from Apollo (filtered: agency owners, 11–50 employees, US)
→ GPT-4o generates a 2-sentence opener citing their most recent LinkedIn post or company news
→ Lemlist sends via custom domain at staggered intervals
→ Replies route to a Notion CRM with auto-tagging (interested / not now / wrong fit)

Result: 4.7% reply rate on [client name]'s first 300-contact sequence. 14 meetings booked.

The opener is what moves the number. Generic "I noticed you work in X space" gets ignored. A sentence that references their actual content gets a response.

If you're sending cold outreach manually, you're competing at a handicap.
