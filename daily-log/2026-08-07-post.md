## Hook

Your new client signed the contract 3 days ago and still doesn't know what happens next.

## Beat 1 — BEFORE: onboarding by inbox

Most agencies onboard clients the same way: the sales person closes the deal, fires off a "welcome!" email, and then manually loops in the ops team. Somewhere between the handoff Slack message, the "I'll get you the intake form" promise, and the kickoff call scheduling back-and-forth, 3–5 days disappear.

The client is excited on day 1. By day 4, they're wondering if they made the right call.

We saw this exact pattern with a mid-size agency client. 8-step onboarding process. All manual. Average time from contract signed to kickoff call booked: 6 days. Average number of emails touching that process per new client: 14.

---

## Beat 2 — WHAT WE BUILT

An onboarding automation triggered the moment a contract is signed in their CRM:

1. **Trigger**: deal moves to "Closed Won" in HubSpot → workflow fires instantly
2. **Welcome message**: automated message via Slack (or WhatsApp, client-configurable) — personal-feeling intro, sets expectations for next 48 hours, links to intake form
3. **Intake form follow-up**: if form not submitted within 24 hours, automated reminder fires with a direct link and a one-line explainer of why it matters
4. **Kickoff call booking**: once intake is submitted, automated Calendly link sent with 3 pre-selected slots based on team availability — no back-and-forth
5. **Internal handoff**: simultaneous Slack notification to ops lead with client details, intake summary, and assigned account manager — zero manual relay

Total human effort per new client onboarded: one review of the intake form. Everything else runs.

---

## Beat 3 — RESULT

Time from contract signed to kickoff call booked: 6 days → 18 hours. Emails per onboarding: 14 → 2 (the intake follow-up and a calendar confirmation). Client satisfaction scores in the first week went up — not because the service changed, but because the client felt held from minute one.

The agency now onboards 3× as many clients per month with the same ops headcount.

---

## Close

The moment after a client signs is the highest-trust moment in the relationship. Most agencies spend it fumbling through inboxes.

An onboarding bot doesn't replace the human relationship — it protects the first impression long enough for the real relationship to begin.
