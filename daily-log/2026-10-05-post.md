We screened 200 job applicants in 40 minutes.

No spreadsheets. No inbox chaos. No "can you just skim these for me?"

Here's exactly what we built for a 12-person agency that was drowning in hiring:

**The before:**
— 200+ applications per role
— 6–8 hours of manual resume review per hire
— 3 team members pulled into every hiring cycle
— Still making bad hires

**The after (Make + GPT-4o):**
1. Application form submits → triggers Make workflow
2. GPT-4o scores each applicant against a rubric (skills, experience, culture signals)
3. Top 10% auto-forwarded to Notion with a summary + score
4. Hiring manager reviews 20 candidates in 15 minutes instead of 200

Time to screen 200 applicants: **40 minutes total** (setup + review).
Team hours freed per hire: **6–8 hours → 45 minutes**.

The rubric is the secret. Most AI screening fails because the criteria are vague. We spent 90 minutes with the founder building a scoring rubric that matched their last 3 great hires. That rubric is now the brain of the whole system.

If your hiring process still runs on email + gut feeling, this is the most immediate operations fix you can make.

DM me "hiring" and I'll share the rubric template we use.
