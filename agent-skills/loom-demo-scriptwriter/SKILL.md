# Loom Video Demo Scriptwriter

One minute, one clear before/after moment, screen-share direction included — this is a sales tool, not a full build.

## Process
1. Identify the single clearest "aha" moment to demo — usually the exact manual step being eliminated (e.g. watch a lead get auto-scored and routed in real time) rather than trying to show the whole system end-to-end.
2. Script it in three parts matching a natural 1-minute pace:
   - **Open (0-10s)**: State exactly what they're about to see and why it matters to them specifically — reference their actual posting/pain point.
   - **Demo (10-45s)**: Walk through the live (or mocked) system step by step, narrating what's happening on screen at each click/trigger.
   - **Close (45-60s)**: One-sentence recap of the outcome, plus the specific next step (reply, book a call via `calendly.com/leftclick-meeting-30`, etc.)
3. Pair every spoken line with the exact screen-share direction (what window/tab/tool should be visible, what to click) so the recording can be done in one take without improvising.

## Output
```
[0:00-0:10] SCREEN: Upwork posting open
  SAY: "You mentioned leads sit in your inbox for hours before anyone follows up — here's how that becomes automatic."
[0:10-0:45] SCREEN: n8n canvas, then live run
  SAY: "This webhook fires the second a form's submitted... [narrate each node as it executes]..."
[0:45-1:00] SCREEN: Slack notification landing
  SAY: "That's the whole loop, lead to routed rep in under 10 seconds. Happy to build this for you — grab a time at the link below if you want to go deeper."
```
