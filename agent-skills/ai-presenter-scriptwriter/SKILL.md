# AI Presenter Scriptwriter

Short-form, hook-first, one service per script.

## Process
1. Pick one concrete service from `../../services.html` (a specific system, not a whole category — e.g. "AI Cold Email" not "Lead Generation Systems" broadly).
2. Write a hook line (first 1-2 seconds) that states a specific, surprising outcome or pain point — no generic "tired of manual work?" openers.
3. Write the body (remaining 6-13 seconds) as spoken lines broken into short beats, each beat paired with its on-screen visual/text direction.
4. Close with a specific, low-friction CTA — matching the site's actual CTA pattern (Calendly link: `calendly.com/leftclick-meeting-30`).
5. Keep total spoken word count tight enough to fit the runtime at a natural speaking pace (~2.5 words/second) — count words and check against the target duration rather than guessing.

## Output format
```
DURATION: 10s
HOOK (0-2s): "We cut this agency's lead response time from 4 hours to 90 seconds."
  VISUAL: Before/after timer graphic, emerald accent on the "90 seconds"
BODY (2-8s): "Every lead now hits Slack, gets scored by AI, and routes to the right closer — automatically."
  VISUAL: Quick cut of Slack notification → CRM record populating
CTA (8-10s): "See how at the link below."
  VISUAL: LeftClick wordmark, Calendly link on screen
```
